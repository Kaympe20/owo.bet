Why its easier to just study than it is to go through the effort of cheating

---

<PostDetail>

<details>
<summary>
TL;DR
</summary>
By the time you've put in all the effort to reliably not get caught, it would have been less to just study and you would have gotten better results.
</details>

Studying can feel strenuous for many of us, and we all look for a shortcut. We've all been there; by the end of the first hour, you're only a third of the way through what you have to study and you already feel like you can't take another page. You look up the answers to the test, call it quits, and go to bed. The next you take the test and realize that the teacher has changed the test since the mystery chaotic-neutral individual uploaded their feedback. The realization comes upon you: you're screwed and you should have just done the studying.
